Solar system sizing calculator

Work out how many solar panels and how big a battery a home needs. Fill in the boxes and the answers update as you type.

A kWh (kilowatt-hour) is the unit of energy on an electricity bill. Divide the energy used in a month by 30 to get a daily figure.

Peak sun hours are the hours each day when the sunlight is at full strength. Not sure? Pick an example below.

A watt (W) measures power. Home solar panels are commonly 300 to 450 W.

How many days the home should keep running on the battery alone, with no sun.

Batteries last longer if they are never fully emptied. 80 means only 80% of the battery is used.

Real systems lose about 25% of their power to heat, wiring and the inverter (the device that turns solar power into the kind your home uses). So the sums multiply by 0.75, called the losses factor.
